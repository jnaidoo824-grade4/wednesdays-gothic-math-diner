Special Lesson: Term 3 Fractions & Decimals Made Easy!

🍕 1. Slicing the Pizza (Fractions)

Numerator (Top Number): How many pizza slices you eat.
Denominator (Bottom Number): Total slices in the whole pizza.
Example: Eat 3 slices out of 8 = 3/8!

✨ 2. Magic Equivalent Fractions

Fractions are twins in disguise! 2/4 = 4/8 = 1/2.
If you double both top and bottom numbers, the amount of pizza stays the same!

🧪 3. Decimals (Spooky Tenths)

0.1 is just 1 slice out of 10! 0.5 is half the pizza. Easy as that!
