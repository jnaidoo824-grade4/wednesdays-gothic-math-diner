Grade 4 Term 3: Fractions & Decimals

🍕 1. Pizza Numerators & Denominators

The bottom number (Denominator) is total slices in a whole pie. The top number (Numerator) is how many slices Wednesday eats!
3 slices out of 8 = 3/8!

✨ 2. Twin Equivalent Fractions

2/4 and 4/8 and 1/2 are equal! If you cut a slice in half, the amount of pizza doesn't change.

🧪 3. Decimals (Tenths & Hundredths)

0.1 is 1 slice out of 10. 0.5 is half the entire pizza pie!
